Strategic Bitcoin mining · Qatar

Solar excess.
Bitcoin at a discount.

The mining warehouse will begin operation with the first 20% of the machines online. The miners will be wired directly to the solar farm and utilise excess power the farm cannot sell. The bitcoin accumulated will be managed by the GVNR Fund. At cycle peaks GVNR will look to distribute into USD, harvesting cash to fund the next phase of the operation. At bear-market lows the collateral will be borrowed against, at 20% LTV, using the GVNR CDP Diamond Hands. This raises debt, and the proceeds scale the operation annually until 100% capacity is reached within five years. After that the oldest machines are replaced annually, maintaining operating efficiency.

01

Drivers

The tariff is the price we would pay the farm for surplus power. It is a negotiation, not a published Qatar rate. Uptime of 70% assumes the farm, or a night supply, can keep the machines hashing after dark. Daytime surplus alone would be lower. Qatar retail power has been flat since 2020, so the rise is off. A contract escalator compounds the offtake from March 2027. A 6-hour battery covers the gap between the solar window and 70% uptime, at $250 a kWh, a 2026 utility-scale planning rate. Ten percent of each cohort fails before resale. Working units sell at 12% of the ASIC price in years 6 to 10. Water is $6 a machine a month for the closed hydro loop.

04

Ten-year activity

Each of the first five years installs another fifth of the warehouse. By year five it is full. In year six the machines from year one are five years old and are replaced. The year after that, the year-two machines are replaced, and so on. The building stays full. The fleet turns over.

Stock-to-flow is stock divided by a year’s new supply. A higher number means less new coin against what already exists. The raw model is the published price that ratio implies, not a forecast. Path versus model is this walk’s price as a share of that print. 25% means the coin is trading at a quarter of the model. Hashrate is the network’s work rate, in exahash a second. A policy window is a month the rule allows a sale or a loan.

06

Hashrate and adoption

New bitcoin is issued on a fixed schedule. Hashrate is how hard the network is competing for that issuance. The network finished the third quarter of 2026 near 941 exahash a second. This case starts at 950. In the bear walk the network fades toward 810. In the neutral walk it grinds to about 1,240. In the bull walk it reaches about 1,570.

If the rest of the network is hashing less, this hall takes a larger share of the new coin. That is why the bear walk can mine more bitcoin than the bull walk from the same opening fleet. The extra coin is worth less, because the price path is the weak one.

Stock-to-flow only means anything if buyers take the new supply. The model prices scarcity. It does not create the bid. At the end of September 2026, US spot bitcoin ETFs held about $108 billion. In May 2026 there were about 59 million addresses, and 20,204 holders of more than 100 bitcoin. Those are the adoption facts behind the bull path. They are not a forecast.

08

Payback

The cash clock asks a narrow question: have the dollars actually sold, the harvest, added up to the cheque? At a 25% clip they do not, inside ten years. That is the hold rule doing its job. Most of the bitcoin is kept.

The economic clock marks the bitcoin still held, adds the cash, and subtracts the vault loan. The machines are left out, so a hall full of rigs is not treated as repaid capital. A 12% residual sits beside that number: a salvage allowance of 12 cents on the dollar for the machines still in the warehouse. It is not inside the payback date. The annualised rate is the ten-year compound return on the mark, the cash and the loan. It is a mark, not a distribution.

09

Treasury

Founder class

Portfolio management is 1.5% and 15% of gains. That is the proposed fee for running the bitcoin the warehouse earns. The lock, the notice and the gate are the fund’s standard terms, and they are not agreed for this deal yet. A separate structuring consultancy covers the mining build. That fee is open.

The sleeve

GVNR runs the portfolio the warehouse earns. Spot only: bitcoin, ether, and dollars earning a yield. No futures, no shorts. At least 20% stays in cash. Ether is capped at 32%. The partner owns the warehouse. GVNR operates it and manages the coin. This page is not a subscription, and the split is not agreed.
